# Code of conduct

Triplex is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make space for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, and disclosure of another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions will consider context,
severity, and repeated conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact options on the owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/bjacobso). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.
